## 2. ASC 350-40 REPLACEMENT COST METHODOLOGY

Under GAAP standard **ASC 350-40** (*Internal-Use Software Capitalization*) and **ASC 985-20** (*Costs of Software to Be Sold, Leased, or Marketed*), software assets reaching technological feasibility are valued based on the direct capitalized engineering labor, technical architecture, and system integration required to reproduce the software in identical functional form.

### 2.1 Senior Architect Labor Model ($1.64M Hard Floor)
- **Asset Count ($N$):** 150 custom full-stack applications.
- **Development Allocation per Asset ($H$):** 100 direct senior engineering hours per application (including schema architecture, UI ergonomics, RLS security policies, and test verification).
- **Total Portfolio Engineering Hours:**
  $$\text{Total Hours} = 150 \times 100 = 15,000\text{ hours}$$
- **Benchmark Senior Architect Hourly Rate ($R$):** $\$109.49/\text{hour}$ (fully loaded senior systems engineer wage).
- **Senior Architect Hard Floor Capitalization:**
  $$\text{Hard Floor} = 15,000\text{ hrs} \times \$109.49/\text{hr} = \$1,642,350 \approx \$1.64\text{M}$$

### 2.2 Agency Build-Cost Appraisal ($2.14M Appraisal)
- A boutique software agency charging standard enterprise project fees would invoice between **$10,000 and $25,000** per custom full-stack operating system.
- Blended replacement benchmark across 86 Track 1 Lean prototypes and 50 Track 2 multi-screen SCADA flagships plus 14 Track 3 F1 Skunkworks engines yields an **appraised replacement labor cost of $2.135M**.

### 2.3 The 41% Bulk Asset Discount & "As-Is" Floor ($1.26M Reserve)
- Institutional rollup buyers acquire pre-revenue portfolios at a portfolio bulk liquidity discount.
- Applying a **41% bulk asset discount** to the $2.135M Agency Build-Cost Appraisal:
  $$\text{Reserve Floor} = \$2,135,000 \times (1 - 0.41) = \$1,259,650 \approx \$1.26\text{M}$$
- This establishes the **"As-Is" Bare Minimum Baseline Trim Floor** of **$1.26M**.
