7. Clean demo URL, walkthrough video, screenshots, and concept brief.
8. Standalone exportable codebase, clean documentation, dependency record, and license terms.
9. Prominent "simulated data / not production" disclosure.

## 5. Micro-APA Strategy & IP Transfer Boundaries

A **micro-APA** is a small asset purchase agreement where a buyer acquires ownership or exclusive rights to **one defined asset**:
- **Strict Single-Asset Scope**: A micro-APA transfers defined rights/code to one specific asset. It **NEVER** transfers GhostFactoryOS, Aura & Grid, shared IP, or the full portfolio.
- **Default to Non-Exclusive Licensing**: Prioritize recurring cash flow from hosted leases, commercial licenses, and agency fleet agreements.
- **Permanent Exclusions**:
  - GhostFactoryOS brand, factory prompts, and management systems.
  - Aura & Grid showroom, branding, and dealership portal.
  - Reusable frameworks, design tokens, UI components, and shared source libraries.
  - Deployment pipelines, templates, and operational know-how.
  - Rights to future catalog releases.

## 6. Product Truth Labels & Permitted Terminology

To maintain institutional diligence standards, all public copy and documentation must adhere to standardized product truth labels:

- **Permitted Labels**:
  - `Template`: Source-code starting point for customization.
  - `Interactive Prototype`: Clickable sample workflow without claim of live operations.
  - `Hosted Demo`: Public preview utilizing sample or simulated data.
  - `Simulation`: Illustrative sample data, physics, calculations, or events; not live data.
  - `Deployable Source Template`: Code, schema, seed data, and setup docs ready for deployment.
  - `Customer-Configured Deployment`: Configured and verified in customer-specific environment.
  - `Production Service`: Verified real users, security configuration, backups, and live operations.

- **Prohibited Claims (Unless deterministically proven)**:
  - "Production-ready", "Enterprise-grade", "Full compliance", "Real-time", "Verified", "Secure", "Audited", "WCAG compliant", "HIPAA-ready", "Financial-grade", "Flight-qualified", "Space-qualified", "Safety-certified".
